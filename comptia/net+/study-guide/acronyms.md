# N10-009 Official Acronym List

Every acronym from the official objectives document (pages 16-17). CompTIA
says candidates should have *working knowledge* of all of them — drill these
with the **Acronym blitz** mode in the study games.

> Note: the official PDF misprints TACACS+ as "TACAS+"; the correct term is
> Terminal Access Controller Access Control System Plus (TACACS+).
