# SY0-701 Official Acronym List

says candidates should have *working knowledge* of all of them — drill these
with the **Acronym blitz** mode in the study games.

> Note: where the official PDF's expansion differs from universal industry
> usage (e.g., CVE), the industry-standard expansion is used here. Entries
> with multiple exam-relevant meanings list all of them.
